// Change colors to red/orange

## 🖼️ Background Recommendations

### Best Background Types

1. **Abstract Patterns**
   - Geometric shapes
   - Flowing lines
   - Particle effects
   - Not too busy

2. **Gradients**
   - Smooth color transitions
   - Dark to light
   - Multiple colors
   - Animated GIFs

3. **Gaming Themes**
   - Game screenshots (blurred)
   - Game logos (faded)
   - Character art (darkened)
   - Themed graphics

4. **Server Branding**
   - Server logo (large, faded)
   - Server colors
   - Server theme
   - Community artwork

### Avoid
- ❌ Too much detail (text won't be readable)
- ❌ Bright white backgrounds (text won't show)
- ❌ Busy patterns (distracting)
- ❌ Low resolution images (pixelated)
