# 🎉 Beautiful Role Celebration Messages

## New Celebration System

Users now receive **stunning, personalized celebration embeds** when they earn roles! Each message makes them feel truly special and appreciated.

## Message Features

### 🎨 Beautiful Design
- **Color-coded by role tier** (Green → Red gradient)
- **User's avatar** as thumbnail
- **Server logo** as banner image
- **Professional embed layout**
- **Role-specific emojis and themes**

### 💬 Heartfelt Content
- Personal congratulations
- Gratitude for their time
- Recognition of their dedication
- Acknowledgment of friendships
- Motivational message

### 📊 Complete Information
- Total voice time spent
- Role tier achieved
- Role name
- Special notes for tag holders
